### More visibility, better performance and digital growth

We help businesses improve how their websites are discovered, understood and used.

We combine technical SEO, performance, analytics, content optimization and acquisition strategies to increase digital visibility and attract more relevant users to the business.

### SEO & SEM

Search engines remain one of the main ways people discover websites, products and services.

A digital strategy can combine two main approaches:

- **SEO**, to improve visibility in organic search results;
- **SEM/PPC**, to reach users through paid campaigns.

SEO focuses on building sustainable visibility over time through technical, structural and content improvements.

Paid campaigns can be used to generate immediate visibility, test markets, promote specific products or complement an organic strategy.

The goal should not be only to increase traffic, but to attract people who are genuinely looking for the products or services your business offers.

### How search engines analyze a website

Search engines use complex algorithms to determine which pages should appear for each search query.

Although these algorithms are constantly evolving, several elements can influence how a website is understood and indexed.

We can analyze and optimize elements such as:

- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Page content
- Relevant keywords
- URL structure
- Headings
- Internal linking
- Site structure and number of pages
- External links
- Structured data
- Performance
- Mobile experience
- Accessibility
- Indexability

There is no single formula that guarantees a specific search position.

SEO is about continuously improving the technical and editorial quality of a website and making it easier for both users and search engines to understand.

### Keyword research

A strong SEO strategy starts by understanding what potential customers are actually searching for.

We can help with:

- Keyword research
- Search intent analysis
- Opportunity identification
- Keyword mapping
- Category structure
- Content planning
- Optimization of existing pages
- Identification of new topics and pages

Instead of trying to rank for as many terms as possible, we focus on searches that are genuinely relevant to the business.

### Manual optimization

We do not treat SEO as a simple automated website submission process.

Every website has different goals, content, competitors and technical challenges.

That is why optimization may include a manual review of:

- Priority pages
- Content
- Metadata
- URLs
- Structure
- Internal linking
- Keywords
- Performance
- Technical issues
- Conversion

Tools can help identify opportunities, but decisions should always be made according to the real context of the project.

### Web Development and SEO Quality Assurance

SEO works best when it is considered during website development, not only after the site is already live.

That is why we can integrate SEO best practices directly into the development process.

This includes:

- Semantic HTML
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Clear URLs
- Metadata
- Structured data
- Sitemaps
- robots.txt
- Mobile-first development
- Accessibility
- Core Web Vitals
- Performance
- Internal linking
- Indexability

We also avoid practices that may damage the website in the medium or long term.

We do not use cloaking or other techniques designed to show different content to search engines and users.

The goal is to improve the search experience and help search engines find and understand relevant content.

### Analytics, monitoring and reporting

SEO should be measured with data.

We can configure and monitor:

- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console
- Conversion tracking
